Sunday 22 November 2026, summed up in one image: a broadsheet front page, "The World in One Image", drawn entirely in code — no photographs, no AI imagery. The lead story is the Houthis' missile strike on Riyadh, the second attack on the Saudi capital in three days, drawn as a schematic SVG map of the Red Sea fighting with the intercepted missiles' arc from Hodeida. Around it: the Pentagon curbing defence ties with Canada, Trump vowing a 10% cap on credit-card interest with the incoming Democratic Congress, Rohingya celebrations and Myanmar's dismissal three days after the world court's genocide verdict, Florida's wettest November on record with a bar chart of Melbourne's 22.59 inches of rain, and a numbers strip led by the 6.7-magnitude quake that shook Peru at 00:09 GMT today. Every headline was checked against wire reporting (AFP, AP, Reuters, NOAA) on the day. The page source is `frontpage.html`; `render.cjs` renders it to `entry.png` (1024×1024) with headless Chrome, and `main.ts` delivers the PNG to the grader.
